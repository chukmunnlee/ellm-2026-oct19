# The Monkey's Paw - Reading Comprehension Questions

## Questions and Answers

1. Who are the three members of the White family?
- Answer: Mr. White, Mrs. White, and their adult son Herbert. They live together in a house called Laburnum Villa.

2. What game are Mr. White and Herbert playing at the start of the story?
- Answer: Chess. 

3. Who brings the monkey's paw to the Whites' house?
- Answer: Sergeant-Major Morris, an old friend of Mr. White who had served for twenty-one years in India.

4. Who put a spell on the monkey's paw, and why?
- Answer: An old fakir (a holy man) in India. He wanted to show that fate rules people's lives, and that those who interfere with it do so to their sorrow.

5. How many people can have wishes from the paw, and how many wishes does each receive?
- Answer: Three separate men can each have three wishes.

6. What did the first owner of the paw wish for with his third wish?
- Answer: Death. Morris says this is how he came to own the paw.

7. What does Sergeant-Major Morris do with the paw when Mr. White shows interest in it?
- Answer: He throws it into the fire. Mr. White quickly snatches it back out, and Morris reluctantly warns him to wish for something sensible if he must use it.

8. What is Mr. White's first wish?
- Answer: He wishes for two hundred pounds, the amount needed to pay off the house. Herbert suggests the sum, joking that his father could then be free of debt.

9. What does Mr. White feel when he makes the first wish?
- Answer: He cries out that the paw moved, twisting in his hand like a snake.

10. How is the first wish granted?
- Answer: A representative from Maw and Meggins, the company where Herbert works, arrives to say Herbert was killed after being caught in the machinery. The company denies responsibility but pays the family two hundred pounds in compensation, the exact amount wished for.

11. What is the second wish, and who insists on it?
- Answer: Mrs. White, overcome with grief about a week after the funeral, insists that her husband wish for Herbert to be alive again. Mr. White reluctantly makes the wish.

12. What happens after the second wish is made?
- Answer: Some time later in the night, a knocking begins at the front door. Mrs. White rushes to open it, believing it is Herbert, but struggles with the bolt.

13. Why is Mr. White terrified of what is knocking at the door?
- Answer: Herbert had been badly mangled by the machinery and had been buried for about ten days in a cemetery two miles away, so whatever has returned would not be the son they remember.

14. What is Mr. White's third wish, and what happens after he makes it?
- Answer: The story does not state the wish outright, but it is implied he wishes for Herbert to go away or be dead again. The knocking stops immediately, and when Mrs. White opens the door, the road outside is empty except for a flickering street lamp.

15. What is the main theme or message of the story?
- Answer: That interfering with fate brings tragedy, and that people should be careful what they wish for. Each wish is granted, but in a way that causes suffering.
